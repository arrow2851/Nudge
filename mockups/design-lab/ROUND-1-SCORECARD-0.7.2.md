## Look #1 — Soft Practical Utility

- **Best feature:** Familiar, direct card hierarchy with immediately recognizable controls.
- **Biggest problem:** Repeated cards become vertically heavy as Areas and content grow.
- **Most confusing element:** Count/status chips and overflow menus compete slightly for attention.
- **Most distinctive element:** Practical green utility language and conventional mobile structure.
- **Component worth borrowing:** Area card anatomy, comfortable targets, and simple status summary.
- **Best-performing scenario:** New User and Normal Day.
- **Worst-performing scenario:** Long Content and Large Household.
- **Repeated-use concern:** Familiar but comparatively generic; long feeds may feel bulky.
- **Provisional recommendation:** **Hold as production baseline/comparator.** Do not advance its extrapolated Intervention as an approved design.

## Look #2 — Warm Editorial

- **Best feature:** Strongest combination of warmth, personality, and readable hierarchy.
- **Biggest problem:** Large serif headings consume valuable vertical space under long or enlarged content.
- **Most confusing element:** Small editorial stamps and secondary metadata can require closer reading.
- **Most distinctive element:** Household-journal tone without becoming ornamental.
- **Component worth borrowing:** Typography hierarchy, attention divider, and humane Intervention copy.
- **Best-performing scenario:** Normal Day and Intervention.
- **Worst-performing scenario:** Long Content and Large Text.
- **Repeated-use concern:** Editorial richness could feel slower than more compact systems during frequent scanning.
- **Provisional recommendation:** **Revise or retain as a third finalist.** Strong component source even if not selected as the dominant system.

## Look #3 — Precision Minimal

- **Best feature:** Fastest scanning and strongest dense-data scalability.
- **Biggest problem:** The visual tone can feel clinical compared with Nudge's supportive intent.
- **Most confusing element:** Small uppercase and monospaced operational metadata may demand more attention.
- **Most distinctive element:** Strict grid and cobalt operational accent.
- **Component worth borrowing:** Area table, status alignment, compact metrics, and dense Section treatment.
- **Best-performing scenario:** Large Household, Heavy Backlog, and Long Content.
- **Worst-performing scenario:** New User and emotional calmness review.
- **Repeated-use concern:** Efficiency may outweigh warmth unless tone and spacing are softened selectively.
- **Provisional recommendation:** **Finalist.** Strongest structural foundation for scale and repeated use.

## Look #4 — Zen Focus

- **Best feature:** Calmest and most supportive Intervention, with a clear next useful action.
- **Biggest problem:** The elevated focus card reduces visible information density.
- **Most confusing element:** In All Clear, presenting an available task can still be interpreted as a recommendation despite the softened wording.
- **Most distinctive element:** Progressive emphasis and generous quiet space.
- **Component worth borrowing:** Intervention, focus card, calm status language, and All Clear treatment.
- **Best-performing scenario:** Intervention, All Clear, and Normal Day.
- **Worst-performing scenario:** Large Household and very long task names.
- **Repeated-use concern:** Users who prefer rapid scanning may find the spacing slower than Look #3.
- **Provisional recommendation:** **Finalist.** Strongest emotional and behavioral fit for Nudge.

## Look #6 — Tactile Household

- **Best feature:** Most memorable personality and strongest physical-control metaphor.
- **Biggest problem:** Decorative framing uses substantial vertical space in dense states.
- **Most confusing element:** Multiple labels, stamps, drawers, and raised surfaces compete for hierarchy.
- **Most distinctive element:** Maintenance-board cards, stamped statuses, and tactile controls.
- **Component worth borrowing:** Checklist controls, restrained status stamps, and selected physical feedback.
- **Best-performing scenario:** New User and ordinary household maintenance.
- **Worst-performing scenario:** Large Household, Long Content, and cross-domain Work/Personal content.
- **Repeated-use concern:** Material styling may become visually tiring and less versatile outside household maintenance.
- **Provisional recommendation:** **Components only.** Preserve its strongest controls and status cues without advancing the full system.
