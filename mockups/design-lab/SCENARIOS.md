# Design Lab Shared Scenarios

All active Looks must consume these scenarios without changing counts, labels, urgency, or product meaning. A Look may change placement, typography, density, iconography, and tone only where the meaning remains equivalent.

## Normal Day

**Purpose:** Test everyday hierarchy with a small, believable amount of attention.

**Expected state:**

- 5 Areas
- 3 routines need attention
- 2 affected Areas
- Kitchen has 1 overdue and 1 due-today routine
- Living Room has 1 due-today routine
- Bathroom, Bedroom, and Car have no urgent routines
- Bedroom includes an unconfigured Section

**Intervention:** 7 minutes on Instagram; suggest Wipe the stovetop in Kitchen, approximately 6 minutes.

## Heavy Backlog

**Purpose:** Test urgency, scanning, emotional pressure, and whether a Look becomes punitive when several Areas are behind.

**Expected state:**

- 5 Areas
- 7 routines need attention
- 4 affected Areas
- Multiple overdue routines
- Bathroom contains both overdue and due-today work
- Bedroom contains an overdue routine

**Intervention:** 18 minutes on YouTube; suggest Empty the bathroom bin, approximately 4 minutes.

## New User

**Purpose:** Test onboarding, empty-state tone, and whether the first useful action is obvious.

**Expected state:**

- No Areas
- No Sections
- No routines
- A clear first-Area action

**Intervention:** 5 minutes on Reddit; suggest adding the first useful action.

## All Clear

**Purpose:** Test whether the product still feels useful when nothing is urgent and whether it avoids manufacturing anxiety.

**Expected state:**

- 5 Areas
- No overdue or due-today routines
- Upcoming and As-needed routines remain available
- The Areas overview communicates that the user is current

**Intervention:** 6 minutes on Instagram; offer a low-pressure future action rather than pretending something is urgent.

## Large Household

**Purpose:** Test long scrolling, many Areas, many Sections, multiple attention states, and whether the visual system supports Work and Personal alongside household care.

**Expected state:**

- 9 Areas
- Home, Car, Work, and Personal content
- 8 routines need attention
- Long Area list
- Areas containing up to 7 Sections
- Work and Personal must feel intentional rather than visually out of place

**Intervention:** 11 minutes on Instagram; suggest clearing temporary working files in Work.

## Long Content

**Purpose:** Test wrapping, truncation, dense metadata, long Area names, long Section names, and descriptive Chore names.

**Expected state:**

- At least one Area name spanning multiple lines
- At least one Section name spanning multiple lines
- At least one long Chore title
- At least one Area with many Sections and routines
- Critical actions remain visible and understandable

**Intervention:** Use a long Work task and location to test the most constrained intervention layout.

## Large Text

**Purpose:** Test the audition at approximately 125% interface text scale before formal device accessibility review.

**Expected state:**

- Critical status and action labels remain visible
- Buttons do not overlap
- Long text wraps rather than becoming inaccessible
- The Intervention primary action remains reachable

## Fairness rules

- Every shortlisted Look receives every scenario.
- Scenario data is cloned before rendering so one Look cannot mutate another Look's fixture.
- Round 1 actions are intentionally simulated for every Look.
- No Look receives additional product functionality during the same review round.
- Intentional wording differences must preserve equivalent meaning and must be documented as a tone experiment.
