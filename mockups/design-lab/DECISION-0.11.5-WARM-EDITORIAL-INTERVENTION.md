# Decision — Warm Editorial Intervention-to-action

**Version:** `0.11.5`  
**Date:** 2026-08-02

## Decision

Warm Editorial may provide calm practical context around an intervention suggestion, but the interaction remains a direct optional action choice rather than a journaling or reflection workflow.

## Accepted presentation

- Serif-led headings and restrained explanatory copy.
- One featured practical suggestion.
- Factual app, pause, estimate, position, and phase details.
- Available Note, In Progress, Completed, and Set Aside states.
- Direct Start, Next, Dismiss, Complete, Reopen, Undo, Resume, and Return actions.

## Prohibited expansion

- Reflection questions or emotional check-ins.
- Notes, diary, or journal fields.
- Requiring the user to explain dismissal or completion.
- Treating continued app use as avoidance or failure.
- Points, streaks, scoring, rankings, or performance measurement.
- Silent production Task creation.

## Rationale

Warm Editorial is strongest when it makes household care understandable and humane. Requiring writing would change the feature from a low-friction nudge into an additional obligation and would break parity with the other pure-Look implementations.
