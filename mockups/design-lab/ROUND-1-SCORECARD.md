## Rating anchors

- **1 — Poor:** actively gets in the way or fails the criterion.
- **2 — Weak:** understandable, but creates recurring friction or doubt.
- **3 — Adequate:** works without a strong advantage or serious problem.
- **4 — Strong:** clearly supports the criterion with only minor concerns.
- **5 — Excellent:** distinctive, dependable, and preferred for repeated use.

Do not average scores until the qualitative notes are complete. A high total does not automatically override a serious accessibility, scalability, or Intervention concern.
